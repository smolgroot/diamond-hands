# Controls

| Button | Action |
| ------ | ------ |
| D-pad | Move and aim: Up, Up+forward; in the air also Down, Down+forward |
| A | Jump (hold for higher); keep holding to hover on the jetpack |
| B | Fire; hold and release for a charged beam |
| L or R | Hold to plant your feet, so the d-pad only aims |
| Start | Leaves the splash; pause; skips dialogue |

## Tips

- You can **stomp** bots by landing on them.
- A **!** over a bot means it is about to attack.
- Touch a **beacon** to make it your respawn point. Retries are unlimited.
- Letting go of A saves the rest of your jetpack fuel; landing or stomping refuels it.
- The upper ledges hold the most diamonds.
- **Options** sets the difficulty: Chill, Normal or Degen. It is saved with your best score.
